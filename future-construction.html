Building Our Monastery

The Monastery of Abuna Hara Dengeel has acquired a 10-acre property in Crandall, Texas. Development of our permanent spiritual and community campus will begin with Phase I improvements and continue through future construction as funding becomes available.

Our Campus Vision

  • A worship-centered Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo monastery campus rooted in prayer, liturgy, and hospitality.
  • A permanent church and monastery serving the spiritual life of the faithful and future generations.
  • Monks’ residence, senior care facilities, guest accommodations, meeting and educational spaces.
  • Youth theological education and AI/technology learning opportunities, including programs for those in need.
  • Farming and beekeeping to support the monastery’s long-term sustainability.
  • A dedicated burial area and peaceful spaces for spiritual retreat, prayer, and community life.
