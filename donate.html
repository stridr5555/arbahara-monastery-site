Help Us Build Our Monastery

The Monastery of Abuna Hara Dengeel has acquired a 10-acre property in Crandall, Texas. Your gift will support the development of our permanent spiritual and community campus, beginning with Phase I improvements and continuing with future construction as funding becomes available.

Ways to give

Support the monastery build fund

With the property secured, every contribution helps move the monastery from vision to reality. Gifts support immediate Phase I priorities and lay the foundation for future construction as resources allow.

Give to the build fund

Use Zelle to contribute directly to Phase I property improvements and the monastery's long-term campus development.

Join as a monthly supporter

Become part of the core group helping carry the construction campaign forward month by month.

What donations fund

Construction-stage needs

Site readiness

Surveying, access planning, site work, and the foundational improvements needed to prepare the 10-acre Crandall property for development.

Professional planning

Engineering, architecture, legal and permitting work, and the technical steps required before major building begins.

Future construction

Spaces for worship, gathering, hospitality, and monastery life, developed in stages as funding becomes available.
