# Family membership and spouse connections

Membership is **$20 per family per Gregorian month**, or $240 for 12 months, after approval. Exempt families remain exempt. It is never $20 per spouse.

Members use separate verified sign-ins and save their membership profiles. Under **Family connection**, one spouse enters the other's sign-in email or phone and confirms the sharing notice. Email invitations appear privately in the matching verified account; this release does not send email/SMS notifications. Requests expire after 14 days. Phone requests require an administrator or secretary to independently verify identity and phone ownership and match the spouse's verified account. Editing a profile phone does not prove ownership. The recipient must still confirm in their own account.

Both consent to sharing the current household's membership, address, dues coverage and household collection records. Personal giving, passwords, roles and full bank details remain separate. Neither spouse gains staff permissions. Requests do not disclose whether an account exists. Declines do not disclose the target's profile name. Either spouse can cancel a connection awaiting household review; ending an already linked household requires office assistance.

## Consolidation

- An empty, unapproved, single-account application can be retired and its account linked to the other household after mutual consent. No charge is created by linking. Approval and the existing dues engine assess one household, not each account.
- Other accounts, approval, financial history or bank requests require office review. No automatic payment migration or cancellation occurs.
- In **Family connections**, review both household records and select the approved primary family. Reviewed consolidation starts next month (Texas time). It blocks historical unpaid dues, future prepaid dues, unapplied dues credits, other linked accounts and active secondary ACH setup. Resolve these through existing accountant workflows first; no automatic refund or gift conversion occurs.
- Eligible unpaid future duplicate assessments are reversed with balanced journal entries on their original dates, not deleted. Closed-period controls still apply. The secondary household becomes inactive so recurring assessment does not create new charges there.
- Original receipts and historical allocations are not moved. The secondary member retains access to their own previous household records and personal export. These former records are not shared with their spouse.
- Cancelling or withdrawing a website bank setup request does **not** cancel Chase instructions. The office must resolve any duplicate actual bank instruction separately before consolidation. No transaction is initiated by these features.

Phone verification and consolidation are audited. Finance staff can inspect the queue; only existing administrators/secretaries can perform these membership changes. No new security roles are granted.
