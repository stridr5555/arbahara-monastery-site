# Arbahara website and member portal

## Approved request

Preserve the ivory, terracotta, and dark-brown palette. Rebuild the public website,
integrate the supplied pastoral donation appeal, and add Convex member sign-in,
membership tracking, ACH and Zelle giving, event QR tickets, and a lasting archive.
Use Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo sources only for faith content. Preserve recordings,
site plans, property photographs, public contact details, and existing store routes.

## Delivery

1. Research and content inventory; verify service accounts and receiving account.
2. Public multipage redesign with static HTML, responsive navigation, reduced motion,
   clear worship/visitor information, construction plans, learning, archive, and giving.
3. Convex authentication, member profiles, review workflow, private giving ledger,
   event registration and ticket check-in, administrative publishing and exports.
4. Server-authoritative payment tracking, webhook signature verification, idempotency,
   delayed ACH settlement, and manual Zelle reconciliation by authorized treasurers.
5. Automated authorization/payment tests, browser desktop/mobile/accessibility checks,
   production verification, operational documentation, commit, and push.

## Design system

Ivory #f8f5f0; linen #fff9f4; ink #1c1b18; clay #c65b25. Darker clay for accessible
small text. Literary serif display paired with Inter. Fine rules, restrained square
buttons and spacious editorial layouts. The user revised the home direction to
restore the original sacred-image slideshow, original gallery, and spiritual
emphasis. That request supersedes the generated property-photo hero concept.
Use the original Christ icon, sacred portrait, terrace image, and flowers in the
hero, and restore the entire original gallery. Add controllable hero transitions,
scroll storytelling, microinteractions, and CSS 3D depth with reduced-motion support.
No synthetic image may be presented as the monastery's property.
Public controls use native HTML. Archive uses searchable rows and media lists.
Portal uses concise forms, tables, status labels, and single-purpose panels.

## Content boundaries

User-confirmed official name: Monastery of Abuna Hara Dengeel; retain Arbahara brand.
Ten-acre acquisition and property descriptions come from owner-provided content.
Assembly hall, permanent church, residences, education, farming, beekeeping, burial
area, and well development are plans, not claims of existing services or approvals.
No unverified service times, clergy names, tax status, donation totals, event dates,
or project completion claims. Pastoral instructions remain with the monastery clergy.

## Operational boundaries

Stripe receiving account must match the owner's authorization before live ACH is
enabled. Zelle reports remain unconfirmed until treasury review. Membership status
does not depend on a donation. Event admission is distinct from worship attendance.
Keep financial/personal records private; public archive contains approved material.
Use portable data exports, documented restore procedures, and versioned content.
